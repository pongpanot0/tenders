Based on available notice text
Ministry of Health seeks a cloud-based case management platform to streamline patient intake, assessment, and follow-up workflows across 45 clinics nationwide. The platform must integrate with existing EHR systems, support multi-language UI, and provide role-based access controls. Implementation must be completed within 6 months of contract award. Current systems are legacy on-premise; migration assistance required.
